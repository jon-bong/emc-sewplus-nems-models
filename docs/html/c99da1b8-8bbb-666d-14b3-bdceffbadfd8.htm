    Stl038Report Class

    Represents a report that contains a market participant’s details for metering error adjustment amounts for load Market Participants that withdraw energy from the Grid containing the changed WEQ, WMQ, WFQ and WDQ quantities and the Metering Error Adjustments for the Settlement Account broken down by period. The display of the PSOAdmin charges and EMCAdmin charges are up to 4 decimal places.
